Select a source and search. Turn on painting to edit regions here; colored borders show your paint over the heatmap.

With paint constraints, candidates keep the source’s painted component and avoid other colors. Results are conditional. With paint constraints enabled, painting keeps the last search origin pinned and reruns the search.

Heatmap: pale = fewer survivors, orange = more. Percentages are observed candidate membership, not solution probabilities. Choose an area from 1 to 121. Larger searches remain capped at 8 seconds and may sample only a small fraction of shapes.

Typing previews your number. Enter commits it. Tab / Shift+Tab moves between squares; previews stay until committed. Escape cancels a preview.
